# RecoverFlow AI 💳

## Intelligent Payment Failure Recovery Agent

RecoverFlow AI is an intelligent payment revenue recovery agent built for the **Razorpay AI Buildathon — Track 3: AI Revenue Recovery**.

The system analyzes failed payments, estimates recovery opportunities, prioritizes revenue at risk, recommends appropriate recovery actions, executes bounded recovery workflows in safe demo mode, and maintains an auditable record of recovery actions.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Failed payments create significant revenue leakage for businesses.

Not every payment failure should be handled in the same way.

For example:

- Temporary bank failures may be retried later.
- Network timeouts may require a delayed retry.
- Insufficient-funds failures may require a later retry.
- Expired payment methods require a payment-link recovery.
- Repeated failures should be escalated for manual review.

RecoverFlow AI automatically analyzes these situations and selects an appropriate recovery strategy.

---

## 💡 Solution

RecoverFlow AI follows an intelligent payment recovery workflow:

```text
Failed Payment
      ↓
Failure Diagnosis
      ↓
Recovery Probability
      ↓
Expected Recoverable Value
      ↓
Priority Scoring
      ↓
Agent Decision
      ↓

      ↓
